# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-25)
First release.

### Reading
- Browse the Qur'an, Namaz, Dua, Ziyarat and A'maal as in the app. Includes breadcrumbs, previous/next links and the Path to Supplication shortcuts.
- Show or hide Arabic, transliteration, English, Roman Urdu, Urdu, notes, verse numbers and waqf signs while reading. The page restyles in place, keeps your position and shows no reload prompt. Each switch can also be assigned to a gesture.

### Links and search
- 8,518 cross-references are tappable. Verse references jump to the verse, and alias entries open their target page.
- Entries the app hides from its index are still reachable by link and search.
- Full-text search in every language. Arabic and Urdu match without harakat. Results show a snippet, 50 at a time.

### Bookmarks and fonts
- Bookmarks, alongside KOReader's own bookmarks and highlights.
- Qur'an pages show the source's verse numbers.
- Optional Amiri Arabic font.

### On the device
- Long pages are split into parts of about 60 KB.
- The database is closed while you read.
- Modules load on first use.
